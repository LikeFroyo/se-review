# Named gap register

Every candidate idea must cite a row here. A gap not in this register cannot be closed by an
adoption, because there is nothing named to close.

`docs/closure.md` is the handover: what is built, what is measured with its denominator, and
which numbers not to trust. It is linked from here because a document referenced by nothing is
the defect this register was built to catch — the register itself was untracked for exactly
that reason.

**How a row is closed.** A closure is dated and re-verified against the tree, and the register's own
validator enforces both: a row marked Closed without a date, or whose own text argues against its
status, is rejected. Three closures had to be downgraded to Partial when this rule landed, because
they had been recorded with no date and no way to check them; all eleven were re-verified and dated
on 2026-10-05, and one had a citation that pointed at the wrong file. Rows added by the 2026-10-04
pass are marked **NEW**; each was verified against the tree before being recorded, and three candidates
that claimed new gaps did not survive that check and are in the report's drops.
